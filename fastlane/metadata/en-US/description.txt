Travel Spain fearlessly. LinguaViaje is the pocket translator built for food lovers crossing Spain and the Basque Country — point your camera at a menu, hold to record a phrase, or paste any text and get an instant, plain-English explanation of what you are actually about to order.

PHOTO MENU TRANSLATION
Snap a single page or a whole multi-page tasting menu. LinguaViaje extracts every dish and translates each one side-by-side, with a short cultural or ingredient note so you know whether that "morros" is going to surprise you.

VOICE TRANSLATION
Hold to record. LinguaViaje auto-detects whether you are speaking Spanish, Basque (Euskara) or English, transcribes it, translates it, and reads the answer back in a natural-sounding voice — perfect for asking the waiter a question or replying when they ask you one.

TEXT TRANSLATION
Paste a phrase, a dish name, or a whole menu item and get an accurate translation plus a one-line description of the ingredients or origin.

ORDER BUILDER
Tap dishes on a scanned menu to build your order, then play it back out loud in Spanish so the waiter understands exactly what you want.

DISCOVER
Curated food vocabulary for tapas, pintxos, Basque specialties and regional dishes so you can learn before you fly.

HISTORY, OFFLINE
Every translation is saved on-device and browsable offline. Great for the airport, the train, or a mountain village with no signal.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Bring your own OpenAI API key. It is stored in the device's secure enclave and never leaves your phone. No tracking, no accounts, no analytics.

Made for travelers, language learners, exchange students, expats, foodies, sommeliers, and anyone who has ever stared at a Spanish menu and wondered what "callos" really means.
